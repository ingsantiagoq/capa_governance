# Context Policy · Route Conformance

Status: mandatory policy.

Rules:

- One user intent maps to one primary domain expert.
- Impacted domains must be declared before work proceeds.
- Cross-domain risk must produce handoff targets.
- The reviewer checks route, scope, sources, policies and prohibited actions before execution.
- No domain expert may silently assume ownership of another domain's facts or policy.

Verifier cues:

- Missing primary expert escalates.
- Multiple primary experts escalate as ambiguous.
- Undeclared impacted domain or missing handoff blocks route conformance.
